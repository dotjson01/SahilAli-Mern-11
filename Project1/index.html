Popular Tourist Destinations in India

Taj Mahal, Agra

An ivory-white marble mausoleum on the river Yamuna — a symbol of eternal love.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

The Palace of Winds in pink sandstone with intricate lattice work.

Ghats of Varanasi

Sacred riverfront steps leading to the holy Ganges River.

Kerala Backwaters

Lagoons, lakes and canals — perfect for houseboat cruises.

Golden Temple, Amritsar

The holiest Gurdwara of Sikhism — beautiful golden architecture.

Hampi, Karnataka

Ancient Vijayanagara ruins with stunning temple architecture.
